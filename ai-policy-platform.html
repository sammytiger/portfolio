Making healthcare policy legible — so participation is possible.

2023–2024Applied AIFounder · Product Lead
01The Situation

CMS, FDA, and ONC regulatory documents are dense enough that most of the people affected by them — clinicians, patients, smaller health organizations — can't realistically read and act on them in time to participate in the policy process.

02The Approach

A generative-AI platform using GPT-4 and retrieval-augmented generation to translate dense regulatory documents into persona-specific, plain-language explanations, built and led as Founder and Product Lead.

03The Outcome

Policy comprehension time dropped from 2–3 hours to under 20 minutes — showcased at the Microsoft AI Hackathon.

04Working With LLMs, Concretely

The model was the easy part. The product decisions were everything around it: which documents belonged in the retrieval corpus, how personas were specified so a rural health advocate and a health economist got different explanations of the same rule, and what "comprehension" meant precisely enough to measure.

Around the model sat the rest of the product: a feedback-aggregation engine that clustered comments into evidence-backed themes, and an analytics dashboard tracking policy sentiment and coverage gaps — cutting comment-review cycles by roughly 30%. The same tools now run inside my own practice: LLMs don't replace the rigor; they compress the distance between rigor and a shippable decision.
