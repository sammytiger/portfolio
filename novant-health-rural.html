Rural mental health, redesigned from the inside out.

2023Novant HealthLead Service Designer
01The Situation

Behavioral-health crises in rural emergency departments get triaged by systems built for physical trauma, not psychiatric or substance-use crises — and rural hospitals often don't have the specialist staffing to close that gap.

02The Approach

Built a Patient Experience Vision Framework across three rural North Carolina hospitals as Lead Service Designer, grounded in 350+ community interviews and multi-site co-design sessions, culminating in a peer-recovery-coach integration model embedded directly into emergency-department workflow.

03The Outcome

$180K in grant funding secured to launch the peer-recovery-coaching program, measurable improvement in Press Ganey patient-experience scores, and patient-experience steering committees driving system-wide change.

04The Blueprint Was the Product

350+ interviews produce ambiguity unless something forces them to converge. The service blueprint was that instrument: every insight had to land somewhere specific — a moment in ED triage, a handoff, a role, a script. Vision language that couldn't be placed in the workflow didn't make it in.

That specificity is what made the program real. The peer-recovery-coach model could be funded, staffed, and defended precisely because the blueprint answered the operational questions — who intervenes, at which moment, with what authority — before anyone was asked to commit.
