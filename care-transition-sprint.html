02

Why it's missed

Discharge planning treats caregivers as logistics. Rides arranged. Prescriptions filled. Appointments scheduled. Necessary — and not the problem.

Caregiver capacity is a nervous-system problem: chronic load, silent until it isn't. From the hospital's side, escalation looks sudden. From the home, it was visible for days. Nobody was measuring the right thing.
03

The approach

Three moves, one integrated readmissions program.

Map the actual discharge-to-day-30 path inside your system: handoffs, blind spots, moments a caregiver holds a clinical decision without training.

Who is at highest risk of escalation, and when — read from light-touch signals patients already generate. Nothing new to buy, connect, or install.

Low-friction, staff-deliverable interventions triggered when the data says it matters — not on a fixed schedule, not another app.
04

The engagement · 14 weeks

Weeks 1–2

Discovery

Executive alignment, workflow shadowing, data access, target discharge cohort defined.

Weeks 2–6

Proof

50–100 caregivers followed through the 30-day window via light-touch signals plus friction logging. First live readings of caregiver load.

Weeks 5–8

Design

Intervention design tied to the signal: what triggers, who delivers it, how it lands inside existing staff workflow.

Weeks 8–14

Results

Outcome measurement, ROI model, and a publication-ready case study you own and can replicate across service lines.

Phases overlap by design — design work begins while the proof cohort is still running.

Discovery is also available standalone — a two-week engagement ending in a defined cohort, a mapped workflow, and a go / no-go decision you can defend internally.
