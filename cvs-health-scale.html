Case 02 / 06

Behavior change at seventy-million-patient scale.

2019–2021CVS Health · AetnaLead Strategist
01The Situation

A national pharmacy and care-management platform needed to move chronic-condition adherence at a scale where even small percentage gains translate into millions of better-managed conditions — and where behavioral interventions have to survive contact with real pharmacy workflows, not just a pilot environment.

02The Approach

Led behavioral strategy across twelve digital-health pilots spanning CVS's pharmacy and care-management portfolio, designing interventions that worked with existing staff workflow rather than asking for a new one.

03The Outcome

A ~15% year-over-year lift in medication adherence for key chronic conditions, across a platform reaching 70M+ members. The program was ultimately adopted by Aetna Business.

04Twelve Pilots, One Discipline

The design space was mostly walls, and that was the point. Pharmacy teams were stretched thin; the tech stack was fragmented; digital adoption and health literacy varied enormously across 70M+ members. Interventions were designed inside those constraints, not around them — which is why they survived contact with real stores.

The work ran the full loop: ethnographic research and concept testing, design sprints with Aetna care teams, prototypes tested in AR/VR and delivered through SMS and mobile pilots. Twelve pilots meant twelve pre-agreed definitions of "working" — so scale decisions were made against criteria, not enthusiasm.
