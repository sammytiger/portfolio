The through-line

Designing the layer between body and system.

Fourteen years of longitudinal research, health-system strategy, applied AI, and near-future product design.

Safety, trust, and motivation are nervous system events — not metrics to optimize, but experiences to design. Most innovation fails because it doesn't account for how people regulate stress or hold onto motivation when they're frightened, unwell, or uncertain. Care that feels like care isn't a luxury. It's the foundation of adoption.

The gap between what we can build and what people want to live with isn't technical. It's neurobiological.

01 · Research

Four questions I keep returning to.

Emotionally Intelligent Systems

Interfaces that respond to human states and strengthen judgment rather than replace it. How do you personalize without surveillance?

A nurse who gets burned once by a confident system stops trusting the accurate ones too.

Healthcare and Aging Futures

Most people intend to age at home, and payment models are pushing care the same way.

The grab bar goes in after the fall. The house is the last thing anyone designs.

Neurodiversity and Emotional Regulation

Sensory and cognitive difference as a design input from day one, not an accommodation bolted on later.

A system that assumes a steady hand and a quiet room excludes most people on most days.

Adaptive Technologies for Daily Living

Medication, mobility, cognitive scaffolding, and the invisible work that holds a day together.

The pill organizer on the counter is the real interface.

About & contact

The short version.

I design systems that remember the humanity healthcare keeps forgetting. Across Clemson's Center for Success in Aging, CVS Health, Philips, Novant Health, and now SOMA Systems, two theses have held: environment regulates physiology as powerfully as medication, and participation is impossible when the system is illegible. Most of what we've built ignores both.

Research and field papers go out under Kutuhala — Sanskrit, roughly, for the desire to understand. I also run Hacky Snacky, a monthly edge-computing meetup in Boulder, and I'm forming Umwelten Institute.

Open to roles, research partnerships, clinical fellowships, and academic collaborations. Photography commissions, speaking, and short notes all welcome.
